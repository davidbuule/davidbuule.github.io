## 1. Course Overview

Animal Nutrition and Feed Resources introduces students to the fundamental principles of animal nutrition, the classification and evaluation of animal feeds, nutrient digestion and utilisation, and the application of feeding principles in livestock production.

The course equips students with the knowledge and practical skills required to evaluate available feed resources, understand the nutritional requirements of livestock, and formulate balanced and cost-effective rations using manual and computer-based approaches.

## 2. Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, students should be able to:

1. Explain the fundamental principles of animal nutrition.
2. Classify animal feeds and describe their nutrient composition.
3. Formulate balanced rations using manual and computer-based programmes.
4. Apply animal nutrition and feeding principles to practical livestock production.

Practical sessions provide students with opportunities to apply theoretical knowledge through laboratory exercises and hands-on evaluation of animal feed resources. ### 4.3 Student Preparation and Participation

Students are expected to:

1. Read the relevant practical guide before attending each laboratory session.
2. Observe laboratory safety procedures and instructions.
3. Participate actively in sample preparation, laboratory procedures and data collection.
4. Record experimental observations and results accurately.
5. Interpret laboratory findings and relate them to the nutritional value of animal feed resources.
