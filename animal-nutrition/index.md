# Animal nutrition (ASM 72103)

Second-year BSc, semester 1. Lecturer: Buule David

## Learning outcomes
By the end of the course, students will be able to:
1. Explain principles of animal nutrition
2. Classify feeds and nutrient compositions
3. Formulate balanced rations using manual and computer-based programs
4. Apply animal nutrition and feeding principles to practical livestock production
